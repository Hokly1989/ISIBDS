Structural Frame and Connection

Steel buildings are suitable for buildings such as shopping malls, office buildings, showrooms, and car parks that demand large open spaces.

Moment frame systems with fully or partially restrained connections transfer both shear and moment, providing lateral stability to the structure. High tension bolts are controlled by torque wrench to ensure precision and structural integrity.

Composite Slab and Beam Systems

Composite slab and beam system is an economical solution and widely used in multi-story building because it combines structural efficiency with speed of construction.

The system uses metal deck with reinforced concrete, connected by shear connectors that create composite interaction between the steel beam and concrete slab. This composite action significantly increases load-carrying capacity while reducing steel weight and overall construction time.
